# Guidelines for Reviewers

This page explains _what_ and _how_ to review this **Practical Spiking Neural
Networks** book. It also sets the reviewing standards, platforms, and how your
reviews will be acknowledged; note that these details are subject to change. 

## What to review?
**All** content in this SNN book is reviewable. You can review for grammatical 
correctness, technical/scientific correctness, and the programming examples. 
You can also suggest new topics/chapters for this book.

## How to review?
Due to the nature of this SNN book, for the near future, it will simultaneously 
be in two stages: _development_ and _production_ stages. In the ongoing 
development stage, the book will be written on
[GitHub](https://github.com/open-neuromorphic/snn-book) and in the production
stage, a complete version of the book will be hosted on the
[snnbook.net](https://snnbook.net/) website. The _how_ to review guidelines 
reflect this dynamic nature of the book. 

### Reviewing the Development stage
* Each chapter will be reviewed by at least **two** reviewers. 
  - The editors of this book are the default reviewers, and apart from the 
  editors, there will be at least one expert reviewer. 

* All the review activites will happen on GitHub to facilitate easy response,
  addressal, and smooth development.

* The link to any new (non-trivial) GitHub Pull Request will be posted on 
  [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/), such that the interested community 
  members there can join the review discussions happening on GitHub. 

### Reviewing the Production stage
* Any review comments on the existing technical content hosted on the SNN book 
  website -- if reported on GitHub issues or `#snnbook` Discord channel -- will
  be discussed openly on OpenReview.
  - The link to OpenReview discussion thread will be communicated on GitHub
    issues and/or in the `#snnbook` Discord channel.

* The author(s) of that particular content and editors of this book will engage 
  in the review discussion (on OpenReview); others are welcome to join too!

* Once the discussion concludes (on OpenReview) and if the content has to be 
  amended, the development and further reviews will happen on GitHub, i.e., 
  the cycle repeats.

## Acknowledgement of Reviews:
Your reviews during the **Development** and **Production** stages will be 
acknowledged under the **Reviewed By** section of the related chapters, which 
will be viewable on this website (authorship will _not_ be shared). The 
procedure to acknowledge your reviews is as follows:

* Your organized review of the chapters on GitHub during **Development** will be 
  acknowledged in the **Reviewed By** section with your names visible.

* Your (anonymous) reviews on OpenReview during **Production** will be
  acknowledged as _OpenReview_ in the **Reviewed By** section.

  - In case you wish to make your name known and the reviews are substantial
    (subject to agreement from the authors of the relevant chapter and the editors), then your 
    name will mentioned in the **Reviewed By** section.
