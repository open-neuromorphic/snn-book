# Acknowledgements

This book is a community effort. We are grateful to everyone who has helped
shape it -- through writing, development, and support.

## Quality, review, and AI policy
Quality is our first priority. Every chapter passes through three stages before
we consider it stable: an initial draft, peer review by domain experts, and
continuous revision as the field moves.

Human authorship is what makes that work. Our policy on AI is simple:[^ai-policy]

1. Chapters originate with a human idea. The arguments a chapter makes, and its
   framing, come from its named authors.
2. LLM outputs are never a source. Claims are backed by citations, and those
   citations are the source of truth.
3. Every LLM-assisted passage is reviewed and edited by a human for accuracy
   and precision before it reaches you.

[^ai-policy]: We were inspired to write this down by [Hank Green's AI policy for
his own work](https://www.youtube.com/post/UgkxbXRPEpONUqoIf0Zu2OPNaKBtd95IIgxU),
published in August 2026.
