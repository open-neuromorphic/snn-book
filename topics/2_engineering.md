# Engineering SNNs

This topic covers the complete engineering workflow for SNNs: from event-based datasets to practical and high-performance training setups.

## What You'll Learn

1. **Working with spikes**: Describe your datasets using spikes and load it into your training pipeline
2. **Credit Assignment**: Attribute success/failure to network components across time and space
3. **Surrograte Gradients**: Train your spiking neurons with gradient approximations 
4. **Exact Gradients**: Train your spiking neurons with exact gradients
5. **Meta Learning**: Learn to improve the learning in your network
6. **Biologically Inspired Training**: Work with plasticity and biologically inspired architectures
7. **Evolutionary Algorithms**: Evolve networks that continuously improve
8. **ANN-to-SNN Conversion**: How to convert existing ANNs to SNNs
9. **Optimization**: Hyperparameter tuning, regularization, and performance optimization

This engineering-focused approach gives you the tools to build, train, and deploy SNNs effectively in real applications.
