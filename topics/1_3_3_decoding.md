# Decoding
As described in the preface of [Chp. %s](chapter:encdec), Decoding is about
extracting meaningful information from spikes. Depending on the context of the
problem statement and the information required, one can employ different
decoding methodologies, or even design their own. The _two_ common decoding
approaches in SNN literature are: **Rate Decoding** and **Temporal Decoding**. 
Note that when it comes to training SNNs, the choice of decoding methodology and 
of the loss functions (to train SNNs) are strongly interrelated; we will
describe it in the sections below.
