# Preface: Why this book?

As the interest in SNNs and neuromorphic hardware grows, the importance of a solid entry-level textbook grows as well.
Everyone involved with this book has been faced with the difficult question "how do I learn more about SNNs?".
Most literature focuses on either theoretical foundations or specialized research topics, leaving a gap for practitioners who want to build, train, *and* deploy SNNs. This book fills that gap.

The book serves three purposes:
1. Most importantly, it helps you **get stuff to work**. We have gathered what we believe are the most important and useful **practical** insights. There is more advanced material out there. We want you to *hit the ground running*.
2. It's a list of references. We cover a large range of topics. You will likely find many of them to be irrelevant, and the relevant ones to be incomplete. That's fine. We hope this can be your gateway into advanced neuroscience, electrical engineering, physics, computer science, or whatever SNN-related content interests you.
3. It's community-driven and written by a horde of experts in niche topics. We want it to be a living resource that is developed openly, by the community and for the community. And [you can participate](/contributors)!


**What makes this book different:**
- **Hands-on and interactive**: Every concept includes working code examples you can run and modify
- **Bridges theory and practice**: We explain the intuition, then show you how to implement it
- **Hardware-ready**: Goes beyond simulation to deploying SNNs on neuromorphic chips
- **Self-contained**: Each section works independently with minimal prerequisites

This book is for researchers exploring SNNs, engineers deploying neuromorphic systems, and students entering computational neuroscience. If you want to move from reading about SNNs to actually building them, this book is for you.

The field is evolving rapidly. This book provides the foundation you need to contribute to it.
