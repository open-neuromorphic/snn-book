# Preface: Why this book?

As the interest in SNNs and neuromorphic hardware grows, the importance of a 
solid and practical entry-level textbook grows as well. Everyone involved with 
this book has already come across the difficult question "How do I learn more 
about SNNs and neuromorphic systems?". Most literature focuses on either 
theoretical foundations or specialized advance research topics, and moreover,
the literature is distributed and not organized end-to-end. This leaves a gap 
for practitioners who want to learn, build, train, *and* deploy SNNs on 
neuromorphic hardware; this SNN book fills that gap.

This book serves **three** purposes:

1. It helps you *get stuff to work*. In this book, we gather what we believe are 
the most important and useful **practical** insights. There's more advanced 
material out there; however, through this book, we want you to *hit the ground 
running*.

2. It is a collection of (citable) **resources**, which can be your *gateway* 
(especially for beginners) into advanced neuroscience, electrical engineering, 
physics, computer science, or whatever SNN-related content interests you. 

3. It is _community-driven_ and written by a horde of experts in niche topics. 
We want it to be a living **testament** that is developed openly, by the 
community and for the community. And [you can participate](/contributors)!


### What makes this book different?

- **Hands-on and interactive**: Every concept is accompanied by a working code 
example that you can run locally, as well as, modify

- **Bridges theory and practice**: We explain the theories/intuition followed by 
showing you how to implement them from scratch

- **Hardware-ready**: The SNN examples in this book go beyond simulation to 
their deployment right on neuromorphic chips

% - **Self-contained**: Each section works independently with minimal prerequisites

This book is for beginners (with computational neuroscience mindset) interested 
in SNNs, for researchers exploring and advancing SNNs, and for engineers
building and deploying neuromorphic systems. In other words, if you want to step 
_beyond only_ reading about SNNs to actually building them, then this book is 
for you. 

The field of SNNs and Neuromorphic Computing is rapidly evolving, and this book 
provides you the _foundation_ you need to contribute to the _third_ generation 
of neural networks, i.e., SNNs! and build the next generation of AI systems.
Welcome!
