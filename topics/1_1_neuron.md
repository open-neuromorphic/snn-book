# What is a neuron?

---
Wikipedia defines a **neuron** as (accessed: 20th June, 2025):

`An excitable cell that fires electric signals called action potentials across a
neural network in the nervous system.`

Our brain has more than **80 billion neurons**, and they communicate with each 
other via **synapses**. On average, each neuron connects to about **7000** other 
neurons; and estimates say that the human brain has about **100 trillion** 
synapses [@wikibrain]. Note that apart from neurons, our brain also has **glial 
cells**. On one hand where the neurons are excitable cells that enable thought, 
sensation, movement, reasoning, speech, vision, etc., on the other, the glial 
cells support, protect, and nourish neurons by forming **myelin** and 
participating in the brain's immune defense. 

In this chapter, we will study about **Biological Neurons** and **Glial Cells**, 
and their different types. Also, as this is the first chapter, we will define the 
notations for the neuron's stateful characteristics/variables, which we will
